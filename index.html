How we work

Four steps, repeated every morning before the doors open.

Buy the lot

We cup samples blind, then commit to the lots we love for the season.

Roast weekly

Small 5 kg batches, profiled and logged for every origin on the bar.

Dial in daily

Espresso is re-dialled every morning against the day's humidity and age of the roast.

Pour with care

Every milk drink is steamed to order and poured by hand, never batch-made.

A small roastery with a long memory

Ember & Oak began in 2019 as a two-table counter beside a roasting drum. We still roast in small batches, still bake before sunrise, and still know most of our guests by their order.

Our rule is simple: buy fairly from the farms we visit, brew with patience, and keep the room warm enough that people stay a little longer than planned.

Questions we get a lot

Do you take reservations?

Yes — use the form below, or call us. We hold tables for 15 minutes past the booking time before releasing them.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Add items to the cart and pick "Order Now" — during the demo this is simulated, but the flow matches what we run in-store.

Do you have dairy-free milks?

Oat and almond are available for every milk drink for a small upcharge. Soy is available on request.

Is there wifi and plug points?

Yes, both. We just ask that laptops go away at peak brunch hours on weekends so tables stay available.

Can I buy beans to take home?

250 g bags of every origin on the bar are available at the counter, roasted within the last seven days.
